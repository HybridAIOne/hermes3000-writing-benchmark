# Have AI models become better writers since 2023? — Results, October 2026

*hermes3000 Writing Benchmark · experiment of 19 October 2026 · [Deutsche Fassung](ZUSAMMENFASSUNG.md) · [full report](REPORT.md) · [method](README.md)*

**In 2023, stories by GPT-3.5, GPT-4 and Claude passed 2–13 % of fourteen tests of literary
quality. In 2026, stories by today's models pass 74–91 % — GPT-6.1 Sol 91 %, against 92 % for the
*New Yorker* originals on the same plots.** The remaining gap is originality.

## Key findings

1. **A tenfold jump.** Same twelve *New Yorker* plots, same task, same length, same judges:
   2023 models passed 2–13 % of the tests, 2026 models 74–91 %.
2. **Close to the originals.** GPT-6.1 Sol 91 %, Claude Opus 5.5 85 %, Gemini 4 Argon 84 %, Claude
   Sonnet 5.5 83 %, Kimi K3 82 %, GPT-6 Luna 77 %, Hemmingway-1 74 % — *New Yorker* originals 92 %.
3. **Craft yes, surprise less.** In fluency and elaboration every 2026 model reaches over 90 %, as
   the originals do. In originality the originals pass 83 %, Sol 72 %, the others 31–53 %.
4. **No copying.** No 2026 story shares a single eight-word sequence with its original; the
   longest shared run is five or six words of everyday phrasing — as for the 2023 stories.
5. **The judges are calibrated — on older texts.** Against 2,016 expert verdicts from the original
   study, the same judges reproduce the experts' levels for *New Yorker* and 2023 stories. Whether
   experts would rate the 2026 stories as highly is not yet tested.

## Quotable

- "In 2023 AI stories passed one test of literary quality in ten; in 2026 they pass nine in ten."
- "The best AI story writer of 2026 is level with the *New Yorker* on craft — the gap that remains
  is originality."
- "None of the 2026 stories copies its original: not one shared sequence of eight words."

## How it was measured

The story experiment of *Art or Artifice?* (Chakrabarty et al., CHI 2024) repeated with 2026
models: the twelve one-sentence plots of *New Yorker* stories, the original prompt, rewriting until
each story is within 200 words of the original's length. All stories — originals, 2023 and 2026 —
judged by Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 4 Argon with the study's fourteen yes/no tests;
a test counts as passed when the majority agrees. The judges were first calibrated against the
study's 2,016 expert verdicts.

## Limits

The verdicts are AI judgments; the judges are themselves 2026 models and may favour their
generation's style. A human cross-check of the 2026 stories is the next step. The *New Yorker*
originals are not redistributed; the 2026 stories and every verdict are in this repository.
