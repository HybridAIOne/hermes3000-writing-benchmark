rank across the four tracks; ties were broken by the order of `models.yaml` instead of sharing the
rank. In book context nearly every model scores 100 %, so the models listed first took the better
ranks, and Claude Haiku 5.5 got the sixth seat that belonged to Kimi K3.

Fixed in `c679e04` (with a regression test on the preliminary data). The valid final is
[`2026-10-18-v1-final`](../2026-10-18-v1-final/report.md): the same texts of the six models here,
plus Kimi K3, all judged anew.
